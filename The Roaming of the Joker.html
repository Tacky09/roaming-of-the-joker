        How to play

        New here?Play a short guided round.

        The goal

        You are on a team. Seats take turns around the board, and every other seat is your teammate. Your team wins when every peg on your team is in its finish lane.

        Taking a turn

        1. Draw a card. You hold 5 cards. Tap the draw pile in the middle of the board to pick up your 6th.
        2. Pick a card from your hand. Cards that can't move anything right now are faded.
        3. Pick a glowing peg on the board. If only one peg can move, it's picked for you.
        4. Pick a glowing hole to move there. Red holes mean you'll bump a peg.

        The card you play shows face up on the played pile in the middle of the board so everyone can see it. Changed your mind? Tap the card again or press Cancel card.

        What each card does

        ABring a peg out of home onto your start hole, or move a peg 1.
        2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10Move one peg forward that many holes.
        7Move exactly 7, never more or less. Use it on one peg, or split it between different pegs, like 4 on one and 3 on another. Each peg moves only once. If the full 7 can't be used, you can't play the 7.
        8Move one peg backward 8. Handy for slipping into your finish lane from just past it.
        J, Q, KBring a peg out of home, or move one peg forward 11, 12 or 13.
        JokerJump ahead: move one of your pegs on the track any number of holes forward, not just 1 to 13, up to and including your own finish lane entry hole. A Joker can also bring a peg straight out of home to any hole, with no Ace or face card needed. It can't go into a finish lane, and it never goes backward, so one Joker can't send a peg around the board twice. If it lands on a peg, that peg is bumped, enemy or teammate (a teammate goes to their own lane entry hole and must step in next turn). If that teammate already has a peg on its lane entry hole, there is nowhere for the bumped peg to go, so that move can't be played. Holes with a peg on them are marked for you.

        Playing with the keyboard

        • By default, cards are numbered 1 to 6. Press a number to pick the card and press it again to play it.
        • If the card has only one possible move, it is played right away. Otherwise use the up and down arrows to choose a peg, the left and right arrows to choose a hole, and Enter to play. B jumps to the next peg you can bump.
        • D draws a card and S turns a card sideways. Esc puts a picked card back. You can change any of these keys under Settings, Keys, and the mouse always works too.

        Drawing early

        If you're next, you can draw your card as soon as the current player has drawn theirs. They still finish their turn first, then you play. So it goes: they draw, you draw, they play, you play.

        When the draw pile runs out, all played cards are shuffled back into it. The game uses 2 decks with 4 Jokers.

        Bumping and blocking

        • You can't land on or jump over your own pegs.
        • Land exactly on an enemy and they go back home. Hopping over a peg doesn't bump it.
        • You can't land on a teammate whose lane entry hole is already taken, because their peg would have nowhere to go. Being blocked like this is part of the game.
        • Land on a teammate and they go to their own lane entry hole, where they must step in on their next turn so the enemy team can bump them.

        Finishing

        • Each finish lane has 5 holes. A card too big for the lane can't be used on that peg.
        • Once all 5 of your pegs are in, you keep playing cards on your teammates' pegs.
        • If a 7 puts your last peg in, the rest of that 7 moves a teammate's peg.

        No legal move?

        • One card per turn: if you can't move, turn one card sideways and your turn ends. It stays face up beside your home for everyone to see.
        • With nothing on the track, the turns count 1 out of 3, 2 out of 3, 3 out of 3, and the sideways cards pile up beside your home.
        • On turn 2 or 3 you can play a face card or Joker to come out (your sideways cards then go to the played pile), or turn another card sideways to keep waiting.
        • On the third, a peg comes out for free no matter what, and the 3 sideways cards are shuffled back into the draw pile. If it happens again later, the count starts over.

        Playing online

        1. One person opens Party and presses Create party code.
        2. Everyone else opens Party, types that code under Join a party.
        3. Before creating the party, the host picks 2 v 2 or 3 v 3 and opens Party options to set betting, shuffling, who goes first and more.
        4. Each player picks their own peg color. Empty seats are played by bots.
        5. The host presses Start game. Everyone rolls a die: the highest roll goes first, then play goes clockwise. Ties roll again (or go to the first seat, if the host chose that).
        6. On every screen, your own color sits at the bottom of the board. Playing against bots, there's no dice: you always go first.
